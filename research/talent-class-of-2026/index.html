The industry predicted. We checked.

58%grew HR and talent headcount

The loudest forecast of the year said AI could eliminate as much as half of HR staffing. Across our Class of 2026, 58% grew their HR and talent headcount in the past twelve months and 20% cut it. We graded that call, and the rest of the year's predictions, in the Report Card.

Who we asked.

500

In September 2026 we surveyed 500 HR and talent leaders at US-headquartered companies with 250 or more employees: 100 CHROs and Chief People Officers, and 400 heads of talent and talent acquisition. We expected the executives setting AI strategy and the leaders putting it into practice to see it differently. They were far more aligned than we anticipated. The differences that mattered were somewhere else.

Upper Classmen and Freshmen.

We sorted the Class by how leaders rate their own hiring against their peers on six outcomes. The Upper Classmen, 37% of the Class, rate themselves above average on all six. The Freshmen, 34%, rate themselves above average on three or fewer.

None of it made the headlines in the year's AI predictions.
