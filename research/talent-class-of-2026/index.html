What it is

The industry predicted. We checked.

2026 arrived with big predictions: AI agents running recruiting end to end, and as much as half of HR eliminated. The Yearbook compares those predictions with what organizations report they bought, deployed, and saw in their hiring results.

We surveyed 100 CHROs and Chief People Officers and 400 heads of talent and talent acquisition in September. Interviews with the organizations putting AI into practice fill out the picture.

Research by Kyle & Co, sponsored by iCIMS. Kyle & Co kept editorial control of the findings and analysis.

We expected a gap between the C-suite and talent leaders.
CHROs and talent leaders were far more aligned than we anticipated on adoption, effectiveness, barriers, and priorities.
