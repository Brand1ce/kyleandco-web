Is the AI motion in your operation creating real momentum?

Hi {$name},

Every senior TA leader I'm talking with is navigating a version of the same problem: you're being asked to justify decisions, budget, headcount, technology, on AI returns that few teams have found a reliable way to measure. The pilots are running and the dashboards look busy. The harder thing to answer is the question underneath.

On Monday, November 9 I'm hosting thirty senior TA leaders in a closed-door working session at the Hyatt Regency La Jolla, working that question together under Chatham House rules. It's the first time our AI Momentum Model research gets applied through a talent acquisition lens.

The afternoon

Monday, Nov 9 · 1:00–5:00 PM · Hyatt Regency La Jolla

1:00 The state of play. 5 years of research on the state of AI adoption with inputs from over 1500 senior HR and talent leaders
1:45 Working session: the human line. Speed on the toil is great, speed on the judgment is a risk. What you hand to AI, and what stays human.
2:45 Break. Coffee and something good.
3:00 Working session: when it's wrong. When your AI stack misses, who catches it, who's accountable, and what's recoverable.
4:00 The momentum diagnostic. A short assessment placing your org on the AI momentum curve, and which of the seven drivers is stalling you.
4:40 Close. Each archetype's first moves, and what this room sends into my opening keynote the next morning.

What you'll walk away with

•Your org's placement on the momentum curve, benchmarked against the 350+
•The drivers stalling your progress, and which to tackle first
•A defensible answer to where the human line sits in your operation, and why
•An evaluation standard for the AI you've bought and the AI you're buying
•Three moves matched to your archetype, ready for your team the week you're back

Who's in the room: heads of TA and VPs of talent acquisition at global enterprises, talent leaders at high-growth tech companies, and a handful of directors running AI adoption hands-on. You'll spend the afternoon working alongside people carrying the same scope you do.

The Forum is open to Recruiting Innovation Summit attendees. You'll need a Summit ticket for Nov 10–11 to join us on the 9th. Every application is reviewed to keep the room peer-to-peer, and seats are capped at thirty.
